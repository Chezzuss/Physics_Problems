# Task 02 – String Harmonics

## Problem Statement

A guitar string has length $L = 64 \ \mathrm{cm}$ and fundamental frequency $f_1 = 330 \ \mathrm{Hz}$. The fundamental mode has one antinode. Find the wave speed on the string.

## Theory

For a string fixed at both ends, the fundamental mode has wavelength

$$
\lambda_1 = 2L
$$

The wave speed is related to frequency and wavelength by

$$
v = f\lambda
$$

For the fundamental mode,

$$
v = f_1 \lambda_1
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

Convert the length to meters:

$$
L = 64 \ \mathrm{cm} = 0.64 \ \mathrm{m}
$$

The fundamental wavelength is

$$
\lambda_1 = 2L
$$

$$
\lambda_1 = 2(0.64)
$$

$$
\lambda_1 = 1.28 \ \mathrm{m}
$$

The wave speed is

$$
v = f_1 \lambda_1
$$

$$
v = (330)(1.28)
$$

$$
v = 422.4 \ \mathrm{m/s}
$$

## Final Result

$$
v = 422.4 \ \mathrm{m/s}
$$

## Interpretation

The string wave travels at approximately $422 \ \mathrm{m/s}$. The fundamental frequency corresponds to a wavelength twice the string length.
