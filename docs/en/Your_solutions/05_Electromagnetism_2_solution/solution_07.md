# Task 07 – Wavelength and Frequency

## Problem Statement

The human eye is most sensitive to light with a wavelength of about $550 \ \mathrm{nm}$. What color does this correspond to in the visible spectrum? What is the frequency of this light?

## Theory

The frequency of electromagnetic radiation is related to wavelength by

$$
c = f\lambda
$$

where:

- $c = 3.00 \times 10^8 \ \mathrm{m/s}$ is the speed of light,
- $f$ is the frequency,
- $\lambda$ is the wavelength.

Solving for frequency gives

$$
f = \frac{c}{\lambda}
$$

A wavelength near $550 \ \mathrm{nm}$ lies in the green region of the visible spectrum.

## Step-by-Step Solution

Convert the wavelength to meters:

$$
550 \ \mathrm{nm} = 550 \times 10^{-9} \ \mathrm{m}
$$

$$
\lambda = 5.50 \times 10^{-7} \ \mathrm{m}
$$

Using

$$
f = \frac{c}{\lambda}
$$

substitute the values:

$$
f = \frac{3.00 \times 10^8}{5.50 \times 10^{-7}}
$$

Calculate:

$$
f = 5.45 \times 10^{14} \ \mathrm{Hz}
$$

## Final Result

The color is green.

$$
f = 5.45 \times 10^{14} \ \mathrm{Hz}
$$

## Interpretation

Light with wavelength $550 \ \mathrm{nm}$ is near the middle of the visible spectrum. This corresponds to green light, where the human eye has high sensitivity.
