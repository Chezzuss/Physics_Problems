# Task 10 – Speed of Light in Media

## Problem Statement

What is the speed of light in a diamond, which has an index of refraction

$$
n = 2.42
$$

?

## Theory

The refractive index of a medium is defined as

$$
n = \frac{c}{v}
$$

where:

- $n$ is the refractive index,
- $c = 3.00 \times 10^8 \ \mathrm{m/s}$ is the speed of light in vacuum,
- $v$ is the speed of light in the medium.

Solving for $v$ gives

$$
v = \frac{c}{n}
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

The refractive index of diamond is

$$
n = 2.42
$$

Use the formula

$$
v = \frac{c}{n}
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
v = \frac{3.00 \times 10^8}{2.42}
$$

Calculate:

$$
v \approx 1.24 \times 10^8 \ \mathrm{m/s}
$$

## Final Result

$$
v \approx 1.24 \times 10^8 \ \mathrm{m/s}
$$

## Interpretation

Light travels more slowly in diamond than in vacuum because diamond has a large refractive index. A larger refractive index corresponds to a lower speed of light inside the material.
