# Task 08 – Electromagnetic Spectrum

## Problem Statement

List the following types of electromagnetic radiation in order of increasing wavelength:

- Infrared
- Ultraviolet
- Microwaves
- X-rays
- Radio waves
- Gamma rays

## Theory

In the electromagnetic spectrum, wavelength and frequency are inversely related:

$$
c = f\lambda
$$

Therefore, radiation with higher frequency has shorter wavelength, and radiation with lower frequency has longer wavelength.

The general order from shortest wavelength to longest wavelength is:

$$
\text{Gamma rays} \rightarrow \text{X-rays} \rightarrow \text{Ultraviolet} \rightarrow \text{Visible} \rightarrow \text{Infrared} \rightarrow \text{Microwaves} \rightarrow \text{Radio waves}
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

The requested order is increasing wavelength. This means starting with the shortest wavelength and ending with the longest wavelength.

Gamma rays have the shortest wavelengths. X-rays have longer wavelengths than gamma rays but shorter wavelengths than ultraviolet radiation. Ultraviolet radiation has shorter wavelengths than visible and infrared light. Infrared radiation has longer wavelengths than visible light but shorter wavelengths than microwaves. Radio waves have the longest wavelengths.

Therefore, the correct order is

$$
\text{Gamma rays} \rightarrow \text{X-rays} \rightarrow \text{Ultraviolet} \rightarrow \text{Infrared} \rightarrow \text{Microwaves} \rightarrow \text{Radio waves}
$$

## Final Result

$$
\text{Gamma rays} \rightarrow \text{X-rays} \rightarrow \text{Ultraviolet} \rightarrow \text{Infrared} \rightarrow \text{Microwaves} \rightarrow \text{Radio waves}
$$

## Interpretation

The electromagnetic spectrum can be organized by wavelength or frequency. Increasing wavelength corresponds to decreasing frequency and decreasing photon energy.
